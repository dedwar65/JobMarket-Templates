\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to express my interest in the Managing Economist – AEA 2026 position at Berkeley Research Group (BRG) in Washington, DC. I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at Johns Hopkins University (advisors: Christopher D. Carroll, Nicholas W. Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis), and I expect to receive my degree in May 2026. My primary specializations are macroeconomic modeling, wealth and income dynamics, and computational economics.

My research agenda focuses on the ability of macroeconomic models to generate empirically realistic distributions of wealth and income over the life cycle. A key driver of that work is modeling heterogeneity in the rate of return on assets, and understanding how return dispersion contributes to portfolio accumulation across households. These themes are directly aligned with BRG’s consulting practice, where economic theory, empirical modelling, and data-driven analysis are applied to complex litigation, valuation, regulatory, and competition matters.

In my job-market paper, I calibrate a life-cycle heterogeneous-agent model to match wealth moments from the Survey of Consumer Finances by allowing households to differ in their portfolio returns. The model helps explain observed wealth concentration and return dispersion. In a related research project using the Health and Retirement Study, I examine how trust influences both income growth and asset returns — finding a hump-shaped relationship for returns and characterizing the distribution of the persistent component of returns to net worth. These experiences have equipped me to: (i) frame research strategies and design modeling approaches; (ii) execute econometric and simulation analysis with large micro-data sets; and (iii) translate results into clear, rigorous written deliverables for both academic and practitioner audiences.

I am particularly excited about BRG’s emphasis on delivering high-impact consulting insights that combine rigorous economic reasoning with real-world case execution. My quantitative toolkit (Python, Stata), experience with heterogeneity and distributional outcomes, and my ability to distil complex modeling into actionable insights position me to contribute meaningfully to BRG’s projects.  

I believe I would be a strong fit at BRG because:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item My expertise in simulation-based modeling and empirical validation perfectly aligns with building bespoke analyses in competition, litigation and valuation contexts.
    \item My research focus on heterogeneity in returns, portfolio accumulation and wealth dynamics connects directly to consulting questions around damages estimation, financial behavior, asset valuation and corporate/regulatory strategy.
    \item I bring proven communication and collaboration skills necessary for success in consulting environments—working across teams, presenting to clients, and delivering polished, impactful outputs.
\end{enumerate}

I am eager to contribute my analytical rigor, empirical expertise and modeling experience to BRG’s team and help produce results that matter for clients and case outcomes. Thank you for considering my application.

\closing{Sincerely,}
