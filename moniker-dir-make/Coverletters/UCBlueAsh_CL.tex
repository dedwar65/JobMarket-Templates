\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to express my interest in the tenure-track Assistant Professor position in Economics at UC Blue Ash College. I am a Ph.D.\ candidate in the Department of Economics at Johns Hopkins University (advisors: Christopher D.\ Carroll, Nicholas W.\ Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis), and I expect to receive my degree in May 2026. My primary specializations are macroeconomic modeling, wealth and income dynamics, and computational economics.

My research agenda focuses on the ability of macroeconomic models to generate empirically realistic distributions of wealth and income over the life cycle. A key driver of that work is modeling heterogeneity in the rate of return on assets, and understanding how return dispersion contributes to portfolio accumulation across households.

In my job market paper, I calibrate a life cycle heterogeneous agent model to match wealth moments from the Survey of Consumer Finances by allowing households to differ in their portfolio returns. The model helps explain observed wealth concentration and return dispersion. In a related research project using the Health and Retirement Study, I examine how trust influences both income growth and asset returns. I find a hump shaped relationship for trust and returns and characterize the distribution of the persistent component of returns to net worth. These experiences have equipped me to communicate complex economic ideas clearly, design engaging classroom material, and translate analytical reasoning into accessible instruction for diverse student audiences.

I am particularly drawn to UC Blue Ash because of its strong commitment to student success, personalized support, and teaching excellence. The opportunity to teach Introduction to Microeconomics, Introduction to Macroeconomics, and applied courses such as Economics of Sports or Digital Technologies for Business aligns well with my training and interests. My background in macroeconomics and applied data analysis allows me to connect foundational theory to real world examples, including labor markets, financial markets, and technological change. I value structured and inclusive classroom environments and am comfortable teaching across modalities, including in person and online formats. I am also enthusiastic about contributing to departmental governance, curriculum development, and the scholarship of teaching and learning within a mission driven institution focused on community impact.

I am enthusiastic about living and working in the Cincinnati area and fully engaging in the in person teaching and service responsibilities expected of this role. I value being present on campus, mentoring students directly, and contributing to a collaborative academic community. I am also excited about the opportunity to build a long term academic home in a tenure track position that prioritizes teaching excellence while allowing continued professional development and engagement in research and pedagogical innovation.

I believe I would be a strong fit for this role because:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item I have a strong foundation in macroeconomics and applied economic analysis suitable for introductory and upper level instruction.
    \item I am committed to clear, structured, and inclusive teaching that supports diverse student pathways.
    \item I value service, governance, and professional development as integral components of a successful academic department.
\end{enumerate}

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the mission and student centered community at UC Blue Ash College.

\closing{Sincerely,}
