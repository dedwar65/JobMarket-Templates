\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Visiting Assistant Professor position in Economics at Bucknell University. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and teaching interests lie in macroeconomics, wealth inequality, and quantitative economic methods, and I would be excited to contribute to Bucknell’s strong commitment to undergraduate teaching and intellectual pluralism.

My research examines the dynamics of wealth inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines theoretical modeling with empirical calibration using U.S.\ microdata such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. Through this research I have developed a strong foundation in macroeconomic theory, computational methods, and empirical analysis, and I enjoy translating these tools into accessible insights for students.

Teaching is a central part of my professional development. At Johns Hopkins I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven finance and quantitative methods, where I worked closely with students to build intuition for economic models while developing their quantitative and analytical skills. In my own teaching, I aim to emphasize economic reasoning and real-world relevance while helping students develop confidence in using formal models and data to analyze economic questions.

I would be very comfortable teaching Intermediate Macroeconomics, where I would focus on building students’ understanding of dynamic macroeconomic models and their connection to policy debates. I am also enthusiastic about contributing to courses in heterodox economics or pluralist approaches to economic thought. Exposure to different theoretical perspectives is valuable for helping students understand both the strengths and limitations of economic models, and I would welcome the opportunity to engage students in discussions that connect competing frameworks to contemporary economic issues.

Beyond these core courses, I would be happy to offer electives in my areas of expertise. Possible courses include topics such as wealth inequality, household finance, or computational approaches to macroeconomic policy analysis. These courses would introduce students to modern quantitative methods while reinforcing the broader conceptual tools that economists use to study economic systems.

Bucknell’s emphasis on undergraduate teaching, mentoring, and intellectual community is particularly appealing to me. I am excited by the opportunity to contribute to a department that values high-quality teaching, pluralism in economic thought, and an inclusive learning environment where students from diverse backgrounds can engage with economics.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my teaching and research experience could contribute to the Department of Economics at Bucknell University.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
