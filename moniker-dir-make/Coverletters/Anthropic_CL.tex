\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the position at Anthropic. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research focuses on macroeconomics, economic inequality, and quantitative modeling, and I am particularly interested in applying rigorous empirical and theoretical tools to understand the economic and societal impacts of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence.

My academic work combines theoretical modeling with empirical analysis to study how economic incentives and uncertainty shape long-run outcomes. In my job market paper, I develop a heterogeneous-agent life-cycle model to study the dynamics of wealth accumulation and inequality. The project integrates economic theory with empirical calibration using microdata such as the Survey of Consumer Finances and requires extensive computational modeling and data analysis using Python and Stata. Through this work I have developed strong experience constructing quantitative models, working with large datasets, and translating complex analytical results into clear insights.

I am especially interested in the broader economic and policy implications of AI development. Advances in AI have the potential to transform labor markets, productivity, and the distribution of economic opportunities. Understanding these effects requires careful empirical analysis and economic modeling to inform both policy discussions and responsible technological development. I would be excited to contribute to work that evaluates these impacts and helps guide decisions around the deployment and governance of advanced AI systems.

More broadly, I value research environments that combine technical rigor with a strong sense of mission. Anthropic’s focus on building safe and beneficial AI systems while engaging with the broader societal consequences of the technology strongly resonates with my interests. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my quantitative research skills to this work.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
