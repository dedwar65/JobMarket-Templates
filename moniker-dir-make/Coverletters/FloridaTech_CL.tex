\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the tenure-track Assistant Professor of Economics position at the Florida Institute of Technology. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and teaching interests lie in macroeconomics, wealth inequality, and quantitative economic methods, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Florida Tech’s mission of combining rigorous research with high-quality instruction in a STEM-oriented university environment.

My research focuses on understanding the dynamics of wealth inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines theoretical modeling with empirical calibration using large microeconomic datasets such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. Through this research I have developed strong experience in computational modeling, econometric analysis, and data-driven economic research using Python, Stata, and related quantitative tools. I am particularly interested in applying these methods to understand how macroeconomic policies and financial markets affect household decision-making and economic inequality.

Teaching is an important part of my academic work. At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to build intuition for economic models while strengthening their quantitative and analytical skills. In the classroom, I emphasize clear economic reasoning, applied examples, and the use of data and computational tools to analyze economic problems. I would be excited to teach core courses such as Principles of Microeconomics and Macroeconomics, Statistics, Quantitative Methods, and MBA Managerial Economics. I would also enjoy developing upper-level electives related to my research interests, such as Monetary Economics, Behavioral Economics, or courses exploring wealth inequality and household finance.

I am committed to maintaining an active research agenda that produces publishable work while engaging students in the research process whenever possible. I particularly value opportunities to involve undergraduate students in data analysis and economic modeling projects, helping them gain experience with quantitative tools and research design.

Florida Tech’s emphasis on integrating teaching, research, and applied learning within a technologically oriented academic environment is especially appealing to me. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the department’s curriculum, collaborate with colleagues across disciplines, and mentor students preparing for careers in both the private and public sectors.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I would be delighted to discuss how my teaching and research experience could contribute to the Department of Economics at the Florida Institute of Technology.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
