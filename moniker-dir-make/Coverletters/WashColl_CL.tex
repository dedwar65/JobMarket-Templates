\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the two-year Visiting Professor position in the Department of Economics at Washington College. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research focuses on macroeconomics and wealth inequality, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Washington College’s strong tradition of undergraduate teaching in a liberal arts environment.

Teaching and mentoring students are central motivations in my academic career. At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to develop intuition for economic models while strengthening their quantitative and analytical skills. In the classroom, I emphasize clear economic reasoning and real-world examples so that students can connect theoretical models with practical economic questions.

I would be particularly enthusiastic about teaching Intermediate Macroeconomics as well as introductory economics courses. My research on wealth accumulation and household financial behavior also allows me to connect macroeconomic ideas with topics such as financial markets and monetary policy. In addition, I would enjoy offering upper-level electives such as Money and Banking or Behavioral Economics, where students can explore how economic theory and empirical evidence inform contemporary policy debates.

Washington College’s emphasis on close faculty–student interaction and mentorship is especially appealing to me. I value environments where faculty work closely with undergraduates not only in the classroom but also through advising and informal intellectual engagement. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the department’s teaching mission and to support students as they develop the analytical tools and economic intuition that will serve them beyond the classroom.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
