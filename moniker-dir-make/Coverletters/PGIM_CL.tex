\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to express my interest in the Global Macroeconomic Analyst position at PGIM Fixed Income. I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at Johns Hopkins University (advisors: Christopher D. Carroll, Nicholas W. Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis), and I expect to receive my degree in May 2026. My primary specializations are macroeconomic modeling, wealth and income dynamics, and computational economics.

My research agenda focuses on the ability of macroeconomic models to generate empirically realistic distributions of wealth and income over the life cycle. A key driver of that work is modeling heterogeneity in the rate of return on assets, and understanding how return dispersion contributes to portfolio accumulation across households. These themes align strongly with the mission of PGIM Fixed Income, where rigorous macroeconomic research, global policy analysis, and market-driven insights support fixed income investment strategy.

In my job-market paper, I calibrate a life-cycle heterogeneous-agent model to match wealth moments from the Survey of Consumer Finances by allowing households to differ in their portfolio returns. The model helps explain observed wealth concentration and return dispersion. In a related project using the Health and Retirement Study, I examine how trust influences both income growth and asset returns — finding a hump-shaped relationship for returns, and characterizing the distribution of the persistent component of returns to net worth. These experiences have equipped me to: (i) frame research strategies and design modeling approaches; (ii) execute econometric and simulation analysis with large microdata sets; and (iii) translate results into clear, rigorous written deliverables for academic and practitioner audiences.

What excites me about the Global Macroeconomic Analyst role at PGIM Fixed Income is the opportunity to leverage economic indicators, central bank policy shifts, and global macro themes in a fixed-income investment context. The job posting emphasizes responsibilities such as monitoring and interpreting economic indicators, central bank policy, geopolitical developments, and market trends — and preparing written reports, data analysis, presentations, and talking points for investment teams, leadership, clients, and media. My background in macro modeling and data-driven research gives me the foundation to contribute to these tasks from day one.

I believe I would be a strong fit for PGIM Fixed Income because:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item My quantitative toolkit (economic modeling, simulation, Python/R/Stata) supports the sophisticated analysis required to interpret macroeconomic and market data and inform investment strategy.
  \item My research addresses return heterogeneity, distributional dynamics and wealth accumulation — topics with clear relevance to fixed income markets, duration risk, real-rate forecasting, and portfolio allocation.
  \item I have experience communicating complex technical findings in concise formats suitable for senior leadership, investment committees, and diverse audiences — essential given PGIM’s emphasis on written reports and client-facing deliverables.
\end{enumerate}

I am eager to bring my analytical rigor, economic research background, and communication skills to PGIM Fixed Income and to contribute to insightful macro research that helps guide investment decision-making. Thank you for considering my application.

\closing{Sincerely,}
