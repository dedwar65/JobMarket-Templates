\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Assistant Teaching Professor of Economics position in the Feliciano School of Business at Montclair State University. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My teaching and research interests lie in macroeconomics, wealth inequality, and quantitative economic methods, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Montclair State’s strong commitment to student-centered learning and professional preparation.

Teaching is a central part of my academic work. At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to build intuition for economic models while strengthening their quantitative and analytical skills. In the classroom I emphasize clear economic reasoning, real-world examples, and interactive problem-solving so that students can connect economic theory to practical applications in business and policy environments.

I would be particularly enthusiastic about teaching core courses such as Principles of Microeconomics and Macroeconomics, as well as quantitative or applied courses such as statistics or econometrics. My experience working with economic data and computational tools allows me to help students understand how economic models are used in practice. I would also welcome the opportunity to contribute to graduate-level courses within the MBA program, particularly in areas such as managerial economics, where economic frameworks can help students analyze strategic decision-making within firms and markets.

My research focuses on the dynamics of wealth inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines economic theory with empirical analysis using large microeconomic datasets such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. While my primary focus is research, this work also informs my teaching by providing real-world examples of how economists use models and data to understand economic outcomes.

I am especially drawn to Montclair State University because of its emphasis on high-quality teaching, diverse student engagement, and experiential learning opportunities. I value the opportunity to teach students from a wide range of backgrounds and strive to create an inclusive classroom environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, developing economic intuition, and applying economic tools to real-world problems.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my teaching experience and research background could contribute to the Department of Economics and the Feliciano School of Business at Montclair State University.

\closing{Sincerely,}
