\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Assistant Teaching Professor position in Economics in the Department of Business Analytics, Economics, and Information Systems at Bowling Green State University. I am a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Professors Christopher D.\ Carroll, Nicholas W.\ Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis, and I expect to complete my degree in May 2026. I am enthusiastic about a teaching-focused role that emphasizes high-quality undergraduate instruction within an AACSB-accredited business college.

My teaching experience includes Principles of Macroeconomics, Intermediate Macroeconomic Theory, and applied upper-level courses that emphasize economic intuition, policy relevance, and data-driven reasoning. My teaching philosophy centers on clarity, structure, and active engagement. I place particular importance on helping students with diverse academic backgrounds develop confidence in economic reasoning, especially in core courses such as principles and intermediate theory. These priorities align well with the Schmidthorst College of Business’s student-centered mission and its emphasis on innovative and inclusive teaching.

Although this position does not carry research expectations, my research background strengthens my effectiveness in the classroom. My research focuses on macroeconomics and inequality, using micro data and heterogeneous-agent models to study how risk and economic dispersion shape aggregate outcomes. Drawing selectively on research insights allows me to motivate course material and connect theory to real-world economic questions, without overwhelming students with technical detail. This approach has been especially effective in principles and intermediate courses.

I am particularly interested in Bowling Green State University because of its clear commitment to teaching excellence, student success, and public impact. The Schmidthorst College of Business’s emphasis on Life Design, curricular innovation, and strong connections to the broader community strongly resonates with my own teaching goals. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the economics curriculum, support student learning across core courses, and engage in service that advances the mission of the department, college, and university.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be happy to provide any additional information and would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application further.

\closing{Sincerely,}
