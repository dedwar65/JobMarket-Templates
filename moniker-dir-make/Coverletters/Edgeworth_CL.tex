\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to express my interest in the Principal Economist (PhD – 2026 Start Date) position at Edgeworth Economics. I am a Ph.D.\ candidate in the Department of Economics at Johns Hopkins University (advisors: Christopher D.\ Carroll, Nicholas W.\ Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis), and I expect to receive my degree in May 2026. My primary specializations are macroeconomic modeling, wealth and income dynamics, and computational economics.

My research agenda focuses on the ability of macroeconomic models to generate empirically realistic distributions of wealth and income over the life cycle. A key driver of that work is modeling heterogeneity in the rate of return on assets, and understanding how return dispersion contributes to portfolio accumulation across households. These themes align well with Edgeworth Economics’ emphasis on rigorous, data-driven applied microeconomic analysis in litigation and regulatory settings.

In my job-market paper, I calibrate a life-cycle heterogeneous-agent model to match wealth moments from the Survey of Consumer Finances by allowing households to differ in their portfolio returns. The model helps explain observed wealth concentration and return dispersion. In a related research project using the Health and Retirement Study, I examine how trust influences both income growth and asset returns—finding a hump-shaped relationship for returns and characterizing the distribution of the persistent component of returns to net worth. These experiences have equipped me to: (i) frame research strategies and design modeling approaches; (ii) execute econometric and simulation analysis with large micro-data sets; and (iii) translate results into clear, rigorous written deliverables for both academic and practitioner audiences.

I am particularly excited about Edgeworth’s focus on applying economic theory and empirical methods to high-stakes litigation, labor and employment matters, antitrust cases, and damages analysis. My quantitative toolkit (Python, Stata), experience with complex datasets, and ability to structure and validate models make me well-suited for casework involving liability assessment, class certification, damages estimation, wage and labor analyses, and competition inquiries. I am also enthusiastic about Edgeworth’s collaborative model, where economists lead analytical work closely tied to expert testimony.

I believe I would be a strong fit at Edgeworth Economics because:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item My experience with simulation-based modeling, structural estimation, and empirical validation aligns directly with the rigorous analytical work required in litigation and regulatory matters.
    \item My research on heterogeneity in returns, portfolio accumulation, and wealth dynamics connects naturally to economic questions involving damages, labor market behavior, class-wide impacts, and valuation analyses.
    \item I bring strong communication and collaboration skills—skills essential for supporting expert economists, producing high-quality written work, and delivering clear, defensible analyses to clients.
\end{enumerate}

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my analytical rigor, empirical modeling expertise, and communication skills to Edgeworth Economics’ team. Thank you for considering my application.

\closing{Sincerely,}
