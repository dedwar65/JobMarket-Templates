\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Investment Analyst position at RVK. I am currently a PhD candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University. My training combines quantitative analysis, disciplined data handling, and clear written communication. I am motivated by work that requires accuracy, consistency, and careful interpretation of financial information in support of client decision making. RVK’s client focused investment consulting model and emphasis on teamwork align closely with how I approach analytical work.

My research agenda is at the intersection of wealth inequality and macroeconomics. In my job market paper, I build and estimate a structural heterogeneous agent model with returns that differ across households. The core contribution is to show how heterogeneity in returns and income risk jointly shape the wealth distribution and consumption behavior. Relative to closely related work, my framework performs better at matching the lower and middle portions of the wealth distribution. This difference matters quantitatively. Models that focus primarily on returns heterogeneity can fit the top of the wealth distribution closely but tend to generate too much wealth among the bottom half of households. In my framework, income uncertainty generates a precautionary saving motive that produces genuinely low wealth households. This leads to a realistic distribution of marginal propensities to consume and aggregate consumption responses that align with empirical estimates.

A key feature of my research is careful data collection, reconciliation, and analysis. I routinely work with large datasets, calculate and interpret financial and statistical measures, and prepare tables, summaries, and written explanations of results. I place a strong emphasis on accuracy, peer review, and meeting deadlines. This work requires attention to detail, time management, and the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously, skills that map directly to investment performance reporting and client support.

I am particularly interested in RVK because of its reputation for unbiased investment advice and its collaborative consulting culture. The opportunity to support a diverse client base through performance reporting, policy reviews, and ad hoc analysis is especially appealing. I value environments that emphasize service, professionalism, and adherence to high ethical standards.

My economics training has provided a strong foundation in financial concepts, quantitative reasoning, and data analysis. I am proficient in Excel and PowerPoint and comfortable preparing client ready reports and presentations. I work well both independently and within team environments and take initiative in improving processes and maintaining high quality standards. These skills are central to my research process and would be directly applicable as an Investment Analyst at RVK.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my skills and perspective to RVK. Thank you for your time and consideration.
