\opening{Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Senior Associate position in the Antitrust and Competition Economics practice at Charles River Associates. I am a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University (advisors: Christopher D.\ Carroll, Nicholas W.\ Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis), and I expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and training emphasize applied microeconomics, econometrics, and quantitative modeling, and I am excited about the opportunity to apply these tools to complex antitrust and competition matters.

My research focuses on quantitative economic modeling and empirical analysis using large microeconomic datasets. In my job market paper, I develop and calibrate a structural life-cycle model of household behavior using data from the Survey of Consumer Finances. This work requires extensive econometric analysis, simulation modeling, and programming in Python and Stata, along with careful interpretation of results. These experiences have strengthened my ability to design empirical models, work with complex datasets, and communicate analytical results clearly to non-technical audiences—skills that are central to economic consulting and litigation support.

In addition to my research experience, I have extensive experience programming and implementing econometric analysis using statistical software including Python and Stata. My work frequently involves building quantitative models, performing regression analysis, and presenting results through clear figures and written summaries. I am particularly interested in applying these tools to questions related to market structure, pricing behavior, and competitive dynamics.

I am especially drawn to CRA’s Antitrust and Competition Economics practice because of its reputation for rigorous, fact-based economic analysis in complex litigation and regulatory proceedings. The opportunity to collaborate with leading economists and practitioners on merger analysis, competition policy, and market behavior analysis is particularly appealing to me. I am motivated by work environments where careful empirical analysis directly informs high-stakes decision-making.

My preferred office location would be Chicago. The Chicago office’s strong presence in antitrust consulting and its collaborative professional environment make it an especially attractive place for me to begin my consulting career, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the team there.

I believe I would be a strong fit for this role because:
\begin{enumerate}
\item I bring strong quantitative training in econometrics, statistical modeling, and applied microeconomic analysis.
\item I have extensive experience programming and analyzing large datasets using Python and Stata.
\item I am committed to producing clear, rigorous economic analysis that can support decision-making in complex cases.
\end{enumerate}

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my quantitative training and research experience could contribute to the work of Charles River Associates.

\closing{Sincerely,}
