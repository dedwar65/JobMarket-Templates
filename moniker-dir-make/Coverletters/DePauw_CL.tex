\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the one-year term position at the rank of Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics and Management at DePauw University. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and teaching interests lie in macroeconomics, wealth inequality, and quantitative economic methods, and I would be excited to contribute to DePauw’s strong tradition of undergraduate teaching within a liberal arts environment.

My research examines the dynamics of wealth inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines theoretical modeling with empirical calibration using microdata such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. Through this work I have developed experience connecting economic theory, data analysis, and computational methods to study important macroeconomic questions. I enjoy translating these ideas into clear and intuitive explanations that can help undergraduate students see how economic models are used to analyze real-world policy issues.

Teaching and mentoring students are central motivations in my academic career. At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to develop their analytical and quantitative skills. In my teaching, I emphasize economic intuition and real-world applications while helping students build confidence in using models and data to understand economic phenomena. I also strive to create an inclusive classroom environment where students from diverse backgrounds feel comfortable engaging with economic ideas.

I would be especially enthusiastic about teaching Intermediate Macroeconomic Theory, where I would focus on helping students understand dynamic macroeconomic models and their relevance for policy questions such as fiscal policy, inflation, and financial markets. I would also welcome the opportunity to contribute to courses in finance or financial economics. My research on wealth accumulation and household financial behavior provides a natural bridge between macroeconomics and financial economics, and I enjoy helping students see how these areas connect.

Beyond these courses, I would be happy to contribute to the department’s core curriculum, including introductory economics and econometrics courses, as well as the senior seminar. I value the liberal arts approach to teaching economics, where students are encouraged not only to master technical tools but also to critically engage with economic ideas and apply them to a broad range of social and policy questions.

DePauw University’s commitment to high-quality undergraduate education, intellectual engagement, and inclusive excellence is particularly appealing to me. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to a department that values close interaction between faculty and students and encourages collaborative learning and mentorship.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be delighted to discuss how my teaching and research interests could contribute to the Department of Economics and Management at DePauw University.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
