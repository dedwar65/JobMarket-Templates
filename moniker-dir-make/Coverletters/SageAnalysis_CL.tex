\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for a Management Consultant position at Sage Analysis Group. I am currently a PhD candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University. My training combines advanced quantitative analysis, causal modeling, and structured strategic reasoning. I am motivated by problems that require integrating data, theory, and judgment to inform high stakes decisions. Sage Analysis Group’s focus on advanced analytics, system dynamics, and evidence based strategy aligns closely with how I approach analytical work.

My research agenda is at the intersection of wealth inequality and macroeconomics. In my job market paper, I build and estimate a structural heterogeneous agent model with returns that differ across households. The core contribution is to show how heterogeneity in returns and income risk jointly shape the wealth distribution and consumption behavior. Relative to closely related work, my framework performs better at matching the lower and middle portions of the wealth distribution. This difference matters quantitatively. Models that focus primarily on returns heterogeneity can fit the top of the wealth distribution closely but tend to generate too much wealth among the bottom half of households. In my framework, income uncertainty generates a precautionary saving motive that produces genuinely low wealth households. This leads to a realistic distribution of marginal propensities to consume and aggregate consumption responses that align with empirical estimates.

A key feature of my research is the use of advanced modeling and data analytics to understand complex systems. I work with large datasets, build structural and statistical models, and design analyses to identify causal drivers and evaluate counterintuitive dynamics. I regularly translate technical results into strategic insights that inform decisions. This work requires the ability to move between detailed analysis and big picture reasoning, skills that map directly to Sage Analysis Group’s consulting approach.

I am particularly interested in Sage Analysis Group because of its emphasis on rigorous decision science and its application to consequential problems across industry and government. The opportunity to work in small, collaborative teams on analytically demanding projects is especially appealing. I value environments that encourage asking fundamental questions, adapting analytical tools to the problem at hand, and developing innovative solutions grounded in evidence.

My economics training has provided a strong foundation in data analysis, modeling, and statistical inference. I am comfortable working with Python and related analytical tools and collaborating across disciplines to integrate quantitative and qualitative information. I also value clear communication and teamwork and take pride in producing work that is both technically sound and strategically relevant.
