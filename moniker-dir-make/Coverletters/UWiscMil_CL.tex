\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Visiting Assistant Professor of Econometrics position in the Department of Economics at the University of Wisconsin--Milwaukee. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and teaching interests lie in macroeconomics, economic inequality, and quantitative methods, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to UWM’s strong group of applied researchers.

My research examines the dynamics of wealth accumulation and inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines economic theory with empirical calibration using microdata such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. Through this work I have developed extensive experience with quantitative modeling, econometric analysis, and the construction and analysis of large microeconomic datasets using tools such as Python and Stata.

I would be especially enthusiastic about contributing to the department’s econometrics curriculum. My training and research rely heavily on applied econometrics and computational methods, and I enjoy helping students develop the intuition and technical skills needed to conduct empirical research. I would welcome the opportunity to teach graduate econometrics as well as undergraduate econometrics or quantitative methods courses, emphasizing both rigorous statistical foundations and practical applications using real-world data.

At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to build intuition for economic models while strengthening their quantitative and analytical skills. In the classroom I aim to combine clear theoretical explanations with hands-on empirical examples so that students gain confidence applying econometric methods to real research questions.

I am committed to maintaining an active research agenda and would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the department’s research environment while continuing to develop projects suitable for publication in leading journals. I would also be excited to engage with faculty and graduate students in collaborative research and methodological discussions.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be delighted to discuss how my research and teaching interests could contribute to the Department of Economics at the University of Wisconsin--Milwaukee.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
