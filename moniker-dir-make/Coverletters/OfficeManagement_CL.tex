\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Economist (GS-0110-12/13) position in the Economic Policy Division at the Office of Management and Budget. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research and training focus on macroeconomics, economic inequality, and quantitative policy analysis, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute these skills to the policy work conducted at OMB.

My research examines the dynamics of wealth accumulation and inequality using heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models that incorporate differences in returns to wealth and income risk. This work combines economic theory, computational modeling, and empirical calibration using microeconomic data such as the Survey of Consumer Finances. Through this research I have developed experience constructing economic models, conducting applied econometric analysis, and translating quantitative results into insights relevant for economic policy.

The mission of the Economic Policy Division at OMB—to analyze the economic effects of federal fiscal policies and programs—closely aligns with my interests in policy-relevant macroeconomic research. I am particularly interested in how tax policy, fiscal policy, and financial market conditions affect household behavior and long-run economic outcomes. I would be excited to contribute to research that evaluates the effectiveness and economic impacts of federal policies using rigorous empirical and quantitative methods.

My training has also emphasized collaboration and clear communication of technical findings. I regularly work with large datasets and statistical software such as Python and Stata to conduct economic analysis, and I am accustomed to presenting research findings in ways that are accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences. These experiences have prepared me to work in interdisciplinary teams and contribute to policy discussions across agencies.

I would be honored to contribute to the analytical work of the Office of Management and Budget and to support the development of evidence-based economic policy. Thank you for your time and consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}
