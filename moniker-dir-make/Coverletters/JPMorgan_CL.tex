\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Research Associate position at the JPMorgan Chase Institute. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research focuses on macroeconomics, household finance, and wealth inequality, and I would welcome the opportunity to apply my quantitative research skills to the Institute’s mission of generating data-driven insights that inform economic decision-making.

My academic work combines economic theory with empirical analysis using large microeconomic datasets. In my job market paper, I develop a heterogeneous-agent life-cycle model to study wealth accumulation and inequality, incorporating differences in returns to wealth and income risk. The project requires constructing and analyzing large datasets, implementing computational models, and conducting empirical analysis using Python and Stata. Through this work I have developed strong experience working with large panel datasets, extracting insights from complex data, and communicating empirical findings in a clear and policy-relevant way.

The work of the JPMorgan Chase Institute is particularly compelling to me because of its ability to combine rigorous empirical analysis with unique administrative financial data to generate insights on household financial behavior. I am especially interested in research on topics such as spending patterns, precautionary savings, and household balance sheet dynamics, and I would be excited to contribute to projects that use large-scale banking data to better understand how households respond to economic shocks and policy changes.

Beyond technical analysis, I place strong emphasis on communicating research findings to a broad audience. I enjoy translating complex quantitative results into accessible insights through reports, visualizations, and policy-oriented writing. I would welcome the opportunity to collaborate with the Institute’s research leaders, fellows, and policy stakeholders to produce research that informs both public discourse and economic decision-making.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be excited to contribute to the research mission of the JPMorgan Chase Institute and look forward to the opportunity to discuss my application further.

\closing{Sincerely,}
