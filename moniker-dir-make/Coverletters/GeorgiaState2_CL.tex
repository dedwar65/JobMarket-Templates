\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the tenure-track Instructor, Assistant, or Associate Professor position in Economics at Perimeter College of Georgia State University. I am a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Professors Christopher D.~Carroll, Nicholas W.~Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis, and I expect to complete my degree in May~2026. My academic training is grounded in economics instruction and quantitative analysis, and I am strongly committed to high-quality teaching, student support, and service in a diverse, access-oriented college environment.

My research focuses on macroeconomics and wealth inequality, with an emphasis on how income risk and heterogeneity shape economic outcomes. My job market paper estimates a distribution of heterogeneous returns to wealth using micro data from the Survey of Consumer Finances and embeds these estimates into a structural macroeconomic model. A related project uses longitudinal data from the Health and Retirement Study to study how trust and beliefs interact with portfolio choices and realized returns. While my research agenda is active and ongoing, I view research as complementary to teaching, informing how I present economic concepts, data analysis, and policy tradeoffs to undergraduate students.

Perimeter College’s mission as a multi-campus access institution aligns closely with my teaching goals and professional values. I am particularly drawn to Perimeter’s emphasis on early undergraduate education, applied learning, and student success for a highly diverse population. The Business Administration program’s focus on decision-making, real-world applications, and workforce preparation matches my approach to teaching economics. I emphasize clarity, intuition, and relevance, using data and practical examples to help students see how economic reasoning applies to business, public policy, and everyday decisions.

\textbf{Teaching.} I have experience teaching and supporting courses in \textit{Principles of Microeconomics}, \textit{Principles of Macroeconomics}, and quantitative methods. My teaching philosophy centers on structure, transparency, and inclusivity. I design courses that clearly communicate expectations, actively engage students during class, and provide frequent feedback. I am comfortable teaching in face-to-face, hybrid, and online formats and maintaining a strong on-campus presence through office hours, advising, and student support. At Perimeter College, I would be well prepared to teach introductory and intermediate economics as well as quantitative analysis courses that support Business, Applied Economics, and related pathways.

I am enthusiastic about contributing to departmental and college service, including advising students, participating in curriculum development, and collaborating with colleagues across campuses. Georgia State University’s broader commitment to access, equity, and student mobility strongly resonates with my own goals as an educator. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Perimeter College’s mission and to build a long-term teaching-focused academic career within the University System of Georgia.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application further.

\closing{Sincerely,}
