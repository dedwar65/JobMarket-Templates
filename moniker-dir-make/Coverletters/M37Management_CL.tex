\opening{Dear Hiring Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the full-time Investment Analyst position at M37 Management LP. I am currently a PhD candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University. My training combines mathematical reasoning, quantitative modeling, and data driven empirical analysis. I am motivated by problems that require disciplined evaluation, careful use of data, and clear judgment under uncertainty. An investment focused environment that values rigorous analysis aligns closely with how I approach research and decision making.

My research agenda is at the intersection of wealth inequality and macroeconomics. In my job market paper, I build and estimate a structural heterogeneous agent model with returns that differ across households. The core contribution is to show how heterogeneity in returns and income risk jointly shape the wealth distribution and consumption behavior. Relative to closely related work, my framework performs better at matching the lower and middle portions of the wealth distribution. This difference matters quantitatively. Models that focus primarily on returns heterogeneity can fit the top of the wealth distribution closely but tend to generate too much wealth among the bottom half of households. In my framework, income uncertainty generates a precautionary saving motive that produces genuinely low wealth households. This leads to a realistic distribution of marginal propensities to consume and aggregate consumption responses that align with empirical estimates.

A key feature of my research is the use of quantitative analysis to evaluate tradeoffs and risks. I build models to assess outcomes under alternative assumptions, analyze large datasets, and stress test conclusions. I regularly translate complex information into clear summaries that support decision making. This work requires attention to fundamentals, disciplined reasoning, and the ability to separate signal from noise, skills that are directly relevant to investment analysis.

I am particularly interested in M37 Management because of its focus on thoughtful capital allocation and research driven investment decisions. The opportunity to contribute rigorous analysis in support of long term investment strategies is especially appealing. I value environments where analytical work directly informs portfolio decisions and where intellectual honesty and accountability are emphasized.

My economics training has provided a strong foundation in probability, statistics, and financial reasoning. I am comfortable working with Python, Excel, and statistical tools to analyze data and evaluate investment ideas. I also place a high value on clear written communication and collaborative discussion. These skills are central to my research process and would be directly applicable in an investment analyst role.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute my skills and perspective to M37 Management LP. Thank you for your time and consideration.
