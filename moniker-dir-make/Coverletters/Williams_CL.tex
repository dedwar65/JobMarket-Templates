\opening{Dear Members of the Search Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the visiting faculty position in macroeconomics in the Department of Economics at Williams College. I am currently a Ph.D.\ candidate in Economics at Johns Hopkins University and expect to complete my degree in May 2026. My research focuses on macroeconomics and wealth inequality, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to Williams’s strong tradition of undergraduate teaching and close student–faculty engagement.

Teaching is a central motivation in my academic career. At Johns Hopkins University I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in data-driven and quantitative finance, where I worked closely with students to develop intuition for economic models while strengthening their quantitative reasoning skills. In my teaching, I emphasize clear economic intuition, active discussion, and real-world applications so that students can see how macroeconomic ideas relate to contemporary economic issues such as inflation, financial markets, and economic inequality.

I would be particularly enthusiastic about teaching Introductory Macroeconomics and helping students develop a strong conceptual foundation in macroeconomic thinking. I enjoy presenting macroeconomic concepts through a combination of intuitive models, empirical evidence, and policy examples that encourage students to engage critically with economic ideas. I would also welcome the opportunity to teach intermediate or upper-level courses related to my research interests, including topics such as monetary economics, financial markets, or wealth inequality.

Williams College’s commitment to rigorous teaching within a diverse and intellectually engaged student community is especially appealing to me. I value creating an inclusive classroom environment in which students from a wide range of backgrounds feel comfortable participating, asking questions, and developing confidence in their analytical abilities. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the department’s teaching mission and to support students as they learn to apply economic reasoning to complex real-world problems.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
